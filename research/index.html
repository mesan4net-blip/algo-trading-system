What changed along the way

Every correction made to the method, in order. Most made the results worse — which is the point. A number is only worth having once you know what was wrong with the one before it.

1

First cross-market run

4h base, 20% equity cap, fixed 2-point stop buffer.

QQQ negative, SPY flat, EUR/USD weak. Strong BTC figures were in-sample.

2

Made the settings scale-invariant

Equity cap lifted and the buffer expressed as a percent of price, so one setting means the same on Bitcoin and on EUR/USD.

Comparable at last: BTC +41% over 1.4yr, QQQ -24%, SPY +13%, EUR/USD +10.6%.

3

Swept the smoothing

Every moving-average pair, across all four markets.

No pair generalised. Slower settings flattered BTC and did nothing elsewhere — a clear overfitting trap.

4

Dropped the 1,1 result

A length of 1 disables that smoothing pass, so 1,1 is a plain Heikin-Ashi, not a smoothed one.

BTC's best result was measuring a different indicator. Disqualified; floor set at length 2.

5

Unfroze the slow layer

It had been pinned at 2,2 in every run because that was the chart setting, never because it was tested.

Swung results three to six fold. Grid went from 648 to 3,888 per market.

6

Made the ranking time-aware

Return per year against the worst dip, instead of a bare ratio.

Collapsed an illusion: QQQ's top setting scored 11.5 while earning 0.8% a year over 26 years. Settings that held fell from 468 to 10.

7

Fixed position sizing

Sizes had been rounded down to whole units, forcing one whole Bitcoin per trade and ignoring the risk setting entirely.

BTC halved, from 32% a year to 15.7% — the earlier figure came from positions six times too large.

8

Unfroze the trailing stop

It had been on but pinned to one anchor in all 25,920 runs.

Preference splits by market: trending markets want it on, stock indices want it off. QQQ's surviving settings went from 45 to 258.

9

Charged trading costs

The engine had never applied a spread, while the plan claimed 0.1% per round trip.

The worst finding of the project. EUR/USD fell from 2.4% a year to 1.5% and its surviving settings from 637 to 54. BTC fell from 15.7% to 7.8%.

10

Fixed a look-ahead on fast charts

The higher-timeframe offset was hardcoded at 15 minutes, leaking the daily value early on any faster chart.

Made 5-minute testing honest. Also showed hold-until-stopped turns QQQ and SPY from losing to winning on a 15-minute base.

11

Gave the strategy more ways to end a trade

Seven exits instead of one: hold the break for N bars, price falling back through a trend line, a profit target, giving back a share of peak profit, a time stop, the chart line crossing the mid line, and opposite full alignment.

On QQQ's 15-minute chart a 3R profit target turned -3.0% a year into +3.9%, with the drop cut from 18% to 6.6%. Contradicted the expectation that a target would hurt a trend system.
